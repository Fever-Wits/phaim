# Sources

This file lists URLs, repositories, and texts consulted during
PHAIM development and its associated documentation.

## On completeness

During the work, the following sources have been read. It is
possible that they led to conscious or subconscious associations
in the author or the model. The author does not claim that every
source is mentioned here — it is possible that some sources have
been missed in the course of work.

This is open-source built on mutual respect. If your work or
thinking informed any part of this project and is not credited
here, please get in touch — the omission was unintentional.

Even seeing a title in a search result can lead to activation of
another field, and from there to an idea. Sources are listed
even when only briefly consulted.
